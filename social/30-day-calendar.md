# Edison Tattoo & Body Piercing — 30-Day Social Calendar

How to use this: post one item per day, in order. "IG" = Instagram feed post, "Story" = Instagram/Facebook story, "TT" = TikTok/Reel (pair with the video scripts file). Every caption is copy-paste ready — swap in real photos/videos of your work. Local hashtag set (reuse daily): `#lincolnne #lincolnnebraska #nebraskatattoo #gatewaymall #tattoosnearme #edisontattoo`

**Golden rule:** every post ends in one action — book, walk in, follow, or apply. Don't stack CTAs.
