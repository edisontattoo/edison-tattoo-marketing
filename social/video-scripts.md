# 10 Short-Form Video Scripts — Edison Tattoo

Film all 10 in 2–3 afternoons. Rules that make these perform: hook in the first 2 seconds (say the title line ON camera), captions on everything (many watch muted), native vertical 9:16, end every video by pointing at the screen with the CTA. Post 3–5 per week; the calendar file tells you when.

---

### #1 — "POV: you get your first tattoo at the mall" (Day 2)
**Length:** 25–35s · **Featuring:** front desk + any artist
- [Hook, on camera] "POV: you get your first tattoo… at a mall."
- Fast cuts: walking into Gateway Mall → neon sign → checking in → ID → artist setting up single-use needles ("all opened in front of you — always") → stencil on skin → client approves → needle noise b-roll → done → aftercare sheet.
- Text overlay: "Walk-ins 7 days a week · Mon–Sat 11–9, Sun 12–6"
- [CTA] "Gateway Mall, Lincoln. Come see us."

### #2 — "Questions you're too scared to ask a tattoo shop" (Day 6)
**Length:** 30–45s · **Featuring:** artist, deadpan humor
- [Hook] "You asked Google. I'll just answer."
- Rapid-fire Q&A cut style: "Does it hurt? Yes. How much? Depends where — ask me. Can I bring my kid as reference? Sure. Can I bring six friends? Please don't. Do I tip? If your artist did right by you. Can I get it drunk? No. Next."
- [CTA] "More questions? edisontattoo.net. Or literally just walk in."

### #3 — "Solid gold vs steel — what they don't tell you" (Day 10)
**Length:** 30–40s · **Featuring:** piercer + jewelry case
- [Hook] "Your body might hate your piercing. Here's why."
- Close-ups: steel piece vs gold piece. "Most metal irritation is nickel. It's the most common metal allergy in the world. Titanium: implant-grade, zero nickel, best for healing. Solid gold: 14k, nickel-safe, what you wear forever."
- Show the case. "Most shops in Nebraska don't stock gold. We do."
- [CTA] "Come look at the case — Gateway Mall, Lincoln."

### #4 — "Rating walk-in tattoos we did this week" (Day 13)
**Length:** 25–35s · **Featuring:** artist voiceover
- [Hook] "Rating this week's walk-ins, totally objectively."
- 3–4 pieces, one line each: "Script on the forearm — classic, 9/10. Matching roommate moons — I've done four this month, 8/10. This cover-up — my favorite, 10/10, come get yours fixed."
- [CTA] "Walk-ins daily. Bring your idea."

### #5 — "Things our piercer wishes you knew" (Day 15)
**Length:** 30–45s · **Featuring:** piercer
- [Hook] "Things our piercer wishes you knew."
- 4 quick tips, on-screen text each: "1. Don't twist your new piercing. Ever. 2. Saline, not alcohol. 3. Gold can wait — titanium heals it first. 4. Sleep on it clean, or don't sleep on it."
- [CTA] "Free aftercare guidance with every piercing. Gateway Mall."

### #6 — "Artist reacts to AI tattoo ideas" (Day 19 — highest viral ceiling)
**Length:** 35–50s · **Featuring:** artist + InkBot screen
- [Hook] "I'm a tattoo artist reacting to AI tattoo designs."
- Open InkBot on screen, feed it 3 escalating prompts: normal ("moth, half flowers") → weird ("raccoon Husker memorial candle") → cursed ("a cow that is also a cathedral").
- Artist reacts, then the pivot: "Here's the thing — AI can sketch. It can't read your skin, and it's the average of everyone else's tattoo. I make yours *yours*."
- [CTA] "Bring me your weirdest AI idea. edisontattoo.net/ai-tools — then walk in."

### #7 — "Never do this before a tattoo" (Day 23)
**Length:** 20–30s · **Featuring:** artist, fast cuts
- [Hook] "Never do this the night before a tattoo."
- Red-X graphics: "Drink — thins your blood. Tan or sunburn — we can't tattoo damaged skin. Fake tan — enjoy the orange stencil. Skip breakfast — enjoy fainting in our lobby."
- Green check: "Sleep, water, food, be on time."
- [CTA] "Save this. Then book — link in bio."

### #8 — "Nebraska tattoo law in 30 seconds" (Day 26)
**Length:** 30s · **Featuring:** owner or manager, seated
- [Hook] "Someone just asked me to tattoo them in their kitchen. Hard no."
- "In Nebraska, body art has to happen in a licensed establishment — sanitation, sterilization, records, the works. Home studios and 'tattoo parties' aren't a gray area. They're illegal. And they're how infections happen."
- [CTA] "Licensed shop, single-use everything. Gateway Mall, Lincoln."

### #9 — "We're hiring — and here's the real pitch" (Day 5 or 25)
**Length:** 25–40s · **Featuring:** owner, honest tone
- [Hook] "We're hiring at a tattoo shop in a mall. Before you scroll —"
- "Mall hours mean no 3am club chaos. Our clients are walk-ins, first-timers, and people excited to be here. We built our own AI tools, we stock solid gold, and we train — apprenticeships included. Apply: edisontattoo.net/careers."
- [CTA] "Artists, piercers, front desk. Tag someone who can draw."

### #10 — "Watch a piercing happen (satisfying, not graphic)" (evergreen)
**Length:** 25–35s · **Featuring:** piercer, tasteful framing (no needle entering skin close-up — keep it wide or use the setup/aftermath)
- [Hook] "An ear project in 30 seconds."
- Cuts: jewelry selection from the case → marking placement → client checks mirror → (cut) → finished ear, slow pan → aftercare card handed over.
- Text: "Every piercing includes free aftercare guidance."
- [CTA] "Walk-ins welcome. Gateway Mall, 6100 O St."

## Filming cheat-sheet

- Shoot horizontal lighting behind camera, never behind subject.
- Wipe counters, show the sterilization setup — cleanliness is your #1 differentiator per reviews.
- Get signed permission from any client shown.
- Keep raw clips; re-edit winners into new cuts (a video that performs gets 3 more versions).
- Post at 11am–1pm or 7–9pm local; Lincoln timezone.
