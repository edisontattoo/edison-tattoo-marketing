# Launch & Outreach Kit — Edison Tattoo & Body Piercing

Everything here is designed to be sent by a human (you). Templates are copy-paste; edit the [brackets]. One honesty rule throughout: never write fake reviews, never buy followers. It gets shops banned and outed.

---

## 1. The 5-point reputation engine (do this first)

Your competitors have 200–1,200 Google reviews. You have ~35. This gap is your single biggest marketing lever — everything else works better once it's closed.

1. **Google Business Profile fixes (10 minutes, do today):**
   - Correct the hours (mall listing shows 11–8; your site says 11–9 — pick one and make Google, Bing Places, Yelp, and the mall directory agree).
   - Add 8–10 photos: storefront sign, jewelry case, artist stations, sterilization setup, recent work.
   - Add services list (walk-in tattoos, custom tattoos, piercings, ear projects, jewelry changes) and the booking link edisontattoo.net/book.
   - Post one Google Business "Offer/Update" per week (the blog headlines below work as posts).
2. **The counter ask:** after a happy client says thanks, say: *"You made my week — would you mind leaving that in a Google review? It takes 30 seconds, scan this QR code."* Print a QR to your Google review link and put it at the counter and on the aftercare sheet.
3. **Text follow-up (24h later):** "Hey [name], thanks for coming to Edison today! If you loved your piece, a Google review helps our little shop a ton: [link]. Healed pic? Send it — we feature clients on @edisontattoo."
4. **Respond to every review**, especially the old critical ones: "We've rebuilt our front desk process since 2022 — come give us another try, first consultation's free." Publicly-turned bad reviews are trust ads.
5. **Track it:** goal is +10 reviews/month for 6 months. At that rate you pass every independent shop in Lincoln inside a year.

## 4. The application funnel (getting people to "apply")

Your careers page is a hidden gem — feed it three ways:

1. **Publish the careers blog** ("How to Become a Tattoo Artist in Nebraska" — already written, in /blog) with a link to edisontattoo.net/careers at the top AND bottom. Nebraska apprentice-seekers search for exactly this.
2. **Run a "Show us your book" open call twice a year** — a Saturday where aspiring artists bring portfolios to the shop. Post it on IG. Even applicants who don't get hired become followers and clients. Announce: "Edison Open Call — [date]. Artists, piercers, and front-of-house. Bring your book or your best 5 minutes."
3. **UNL pipeline:** the student-org email above includes apprenticeship language. Art departments are full of people who don't know tattooing is a licensed trade in Nebraska.

## 5. Weekly rhythm (30–40 min/day, or delegate to front desk)

- **Mon:** post this week's calendar item(s); 15 min engaging with local accounts (comment genuinely on Gateway Mall stores, UNL orgs, Lincoln food pages — real comments, no link-spam).
- **Wed:** one Google Business post; respond to all reviews.
- **Fri:** flash-drop or weekend hours story; confirm weekend influencer visits.
- **Sun:** post Sunday-hours story ("open till 6, food court is right there").

## 6. Where to host the blog + hub (GitHub Pages, free)

1. Create a GitHub repo (e.g., `edison-marketing`), upload this folder: `hub/index.html` + the blog `.md` files.
2. Convert blog markdown to HTML (or paste the text into edisontattoo.net's CMS if one exists).
3. Repo Settings → Pages → deploy. Point it at a subdomain like `blog.edisontattoo.net`.
4. Every blog post links back to edisontattoo.net routes — that's the SEO value: content pages feed the booking pages.
