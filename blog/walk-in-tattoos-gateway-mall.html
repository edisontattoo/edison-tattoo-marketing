Walk-In Tattoos at Gateway Mall: How Edison's Same-Day Process Works

Walk-In Tattoos at Gateway Mall: How Edison's Same-Day Process Works

Most tattoo shops in Lincoln run on appointments made weeks out. We're built differently — partly by choice, partly because we're inside Gateway Mall, where "I'll think about it for three weeks" means you've wandered off to buy shoes and never come back. So here's exactly how walk-ins work at Edison, no surprises.

What a walk-in actually means

Walk-in = first come, first served, same-day. It works best for pieces that fit in a session of about an hour to a few hours: script, small blackwork, flash-style designs, most piercings, jewelry changes, and jewelry upgrades.

Big custom work — sleeves, back pieces, large cover-ups — starts with a consult and a booking. Not because we're precious about it, but because a good custom piece deserves design time before the needle.

The process, step by step

  1. Walk in (6100 O St, inside Gateway Mall, Lincoln). Monday–Saturday 11am–9pm, Sunday 12pm–6pm.
  2. Check in at the counter. Tell us your idea, size, and placement. If you have reference images, put them in one folder on your phone before you arrive — it genuinely speeds everything up.
  3. We match you to an artist. Availability depends on the day; that's the honest nature of walk-ins. Call ahead at (402) 326-8571 to check the day's lineup and wait.
  4. Design pass. For walk-in work, artists typically work from your references plus their sketching, right at the station. You approve the stencil before anything touches skin.
  5. The tattoo. Standard session flow: setup (all single-use, opened in front of you — always), stencil, tattoo, bandage, aftercare sheet.
  6. Pay and aftercare. Walk-ins are typically priced by the piece rather than hourly; your artist quotes it at the counter before starting. Deposits apply to booked work, not usually simple walk-ins.

Best times to walk in

From what we see week to week: weekday mornings and early afternoons are the quietest; Saturday is the busiest day in town, bar none, mall-wide. Sunday afternoons fill with "the mall was right there" energy — sometimes the wait is short, sometimes it isn't. A 30-second phone call to (402) 326-8571 saves you a guess.

Piercing walk-ins

Piercings are even more walk-in friendly than tattoos. Our piercer handles everything from lobes to ear projects (helix, conch, daith, industrial), and we stock implant-grade titanium and solid gold jewelry in-house — you pick the piece after we talk placement and anatomy. Free aftercare guidance comes with every piercing; you don't need to buy a care kit of mystery solutions.

The etiquette of walk-ins

Bring a state ID. Eat before you come. One support person is welcome; six is a crowd. Tip your artist if they did right by you — walk-in work is how most artists build their book. And if the wait is long, browse the mall, grab food, come back; we'll hold your place in line by name.

Why we bother with walk-ins

Because a huge share of the best tattoos start as a "hey, do you have 40 minutes?" If you're tattoo-curious and don't know how to start, walking in is the lowest-pressure possible way in. Worst case, you spend ten minutes talking to a tattooer and walk away with a plan.
