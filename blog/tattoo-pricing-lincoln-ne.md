# What Do Tattoos Cost in Lincoln, NE? An Honest Pricing Guide (2026)

Nobody in the tattoo industry loves publishing prices, because the honest answer is "it depends." But you deserve better than a shrug, so here's how tattoo pricing actually works in Lincoln — how every shop in town prices, including us — so you can budget before you walk in anywhere.

## The three pricing models

**1. Shop minimum.** Every shop has a floor, typically in the $60–$100 range in Lincoln for a small piece. A tiny tattoo takes the same setup — needle, ink, gloves, station prep, aftercare — whether it's a cross the size of a quarter or a full hour of work. The minimum exists because sterilization isn't optional.

**2. Piece pricing (how walk-ins usually work).** For walk-in-size tattoos — script, small blackwork, flash — the artist quotes the *piece*: one number, told to you at the counter before anything happens. If the quote changes mid-session, something is wrong; good shops don't do that.

**3. Hourly (custom work).** Sleeves, back pieces, big cover-ups: hourly, typically $100–$200/hr in this market depending on the artist. You'll get an estimate range and a session plan at your consult.

## Deposits — what they're actually for

Custom work usually requires a deposit ($50–$150). Understand what it is: **not a fee — it comes off the final price.** It reserves your artist's design time and your appointment slot. If you no-show, the deposit pays for the design hours they already worked. That's the entire logic, and any shop that can't explain its deposit policy that clearly is telling you something.

## Piercing pricing

Piercings are simpler: a studio fee for the piercing itself plus the jewelry. The jewelry is the variable — basic implant-grade titanium starts reasonable, and solid gold pieces cost more, the way gold earrings cost more than steel ones. At Edison, aftercare guidance is included, not an upsell. Ask any piercer in town what alloy their jewelry is — you'll learn a lot from the answer.

## Why "cheap" tattoos are the most expensive ones

The most expensive tattoo in Lincoln is the $40 one you'll pay $400 to cover up in two years. Cheap usually means: rushed sterilization, artists learning on you, or work that heals blurry. Your skin is not the place to save money — it's the place to save twice.
