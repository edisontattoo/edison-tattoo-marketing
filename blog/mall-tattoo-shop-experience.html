What It's Actually Like Getting a Tattoo in a Mall (Yes, Really)

What It's Actually Like Getting a Tattoo in a Mall (Yes, Really)

When we tell people our shop is inside Gateway Mall in Lincoln, we get one of two reactions. Either "that's genius" or a squint that says a mall tattoo? — like we're running a kiosk that airbrushes names on trucker hats. So let's clear it up, because the mall location is exactly why people who never thought they'd get a tattoo… get one.

The stereotype, and where it came from

The "mall tattoo" suspicion comes from the 90s and 2000s, when a few mall kiosks did cheap, fast, poorly sterilized work. Fair. But a licensed shop that happens to be inside a mall is a different species: same Nebraska licensing requirements, same single-use, opened-in-front-of-you setup, same professional standards as any storefront shop in town — with a food court.

Who actually walks in

A cross-section of Lincoln you won't find anywhere else:

  • The 19-year-old UNL student who's been screenshotting tattoo ideas for two years and needed to just walk in somewhere.
  • Grandparents getting matching grandkid tattoos (yes, really — several times).
  • The guy who came in for phone-case shopping at 6pm and walked out with a piercing at 7.
  • People who are tattoo-curious but intimidated by walking into a "real" shop with no idea what to say.

That last group is the big one, and honestly, they're our favorite. Nobody should need industry vocabulary or a referral from a friend-of-a-friend to get a tattoo. At the mall, you can literally wander in off the street, look at the jewelry case, ask every "dumb" question you have, and leave with nothing but a plan — no pressure, no judgment.

What surprises people

  1. It's clean. Like, conspicuously clean. Cleanliness is the #1 thing people mention about us in reviews, and we're proud of that.
  2. The jewelry case is legit. Solid gold and implant-grade titanium — most Nebraska shops don't stock gold at all.
  3. There's an AI that helps you design. We built InkBot, our own AI assistant, because so many people had ideas they couldn't put into words. Try it on your phone before you come in.
  4. Free parking. Downtown friends, we're sorry. We're not sorry.

The walk-in reality

Walk-ins are first come, first served — the honest nature of it is that Saturday is busy and Tuesday at 1pm is not. Call (402) 326-8571 and we'll tell you the wait like it is. Bring your ID and one reference image folder on your phone; that's the whole prep list.
